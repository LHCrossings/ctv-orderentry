Hiatus
Orders
1. Pick the contract or contracts
Search by code or description. Add as many as the hiatus covers.
2. Hiatus dates
The days that must not air, for example 12/24-12/25 or 12/24, 12/26. A year is not needed; it comes from each contract's flight.
3. Review, then apply
Apply pulls only the spots that sit on the hiatus days (playlist and contract side, with a backup), blocks those days on each line, raises max per day where the short week needs it (max per week does not change), sets those lines to Ready, and adds the reminder to the contract notes.
4. Reschedule in Etere, then check
In the Etere scheduler, schedule these contracts. Only the pulled spots are placed again; the rest do not move. When the scheduler has finished, press Check.
