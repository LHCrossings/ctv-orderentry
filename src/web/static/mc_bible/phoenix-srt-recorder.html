Phoenix TV Recorder on VIDEO-02
Sacramento office · live since 14 Oct 2026 · owner: Lee Hudson

What it does

VIDEO-02 is a PC in the Sacramento office. It receives the Phoenix TV North America feed over SRT and records it to disk, one file per hour.

The recorder replaces the old chain (Datavideo decoder → SDI → DeckLink → Etere Memory on EDIT-02). The VIDEO-02 files are the product. EDIT-02 is being retired.

The recorder runs by itself. It starts when VIDEO-02 boots, restarts if it fails, and needs nobody to be logged on.

Also from the relay: → Live View (VLC on VIDEO-02) · → Confidence monitor (thumbnail + audio meter on the Control Room Monitor Wall) · → Datavideo (SDI out, legacy, for EDIT-02 Memory until it is retired)
One caller only. Phoenix's SRT listener accepts one connection at a time. VIDEO-02 is that caller. Do not point the Datavideo (or any other device) at the Phoenix SRT address. If something else takes the slot, the recorder is locked out and records nothing.

The NAS copy needs a logged-on user

The archive task runs in the CROSSINGSTV\video desktop session on VIDEO-02. The NAS does not accept the computer account. After a reboot, nothing is copied to the NAS until someone logs on as video. The recorder keeps recording in the meantime, and the 72-hour local copy gives the archive time to catch up.

How to check the feed

Control Room Monitor Wall

The "Phoenix TV feed" panel shows a still frame of the feed, taken once a minute, and an L/R audio meter.

  • Normal: the frame updates every minute and the meter moves.
  • Amber: the frame is more than 3 minutes old. The thumbnail task on VIDEO-02 is behind or stopped.
  • Red: VIDEO-02 does not answer. The PC is off, or the network to it is down.
  • NO AUDIO: both channels sit at or below -60 dBFS. The 24-hour strip under the meter marks silent minutes in red.

The meter and frame are taken before the recorder. Silence on the meter means Phoenix (or the SRT path) is silent. Sound on the meter with a mute file means the problem is on our side.

Live View on VIDEO-02

Double-click the "Phoenix Live View" shortcut on the VIDEO-02 desktop. VLC opens the feed a second or two behind air. Use it to confirm Phoenix is sending programming and not its "Daily Talk" fallback. Only one Live View can be open at a time. Opening or closing it does not affect the recorder.

Frames from the last 24 hours

Every minute's thumbnail is kept for 24 hours at http://100.90.230.98:8081/frames/. Use this to find when a fallback or a black picture started.

Things that are normal

  • Reconnects. Phoenix's SRT session drops several times a day. The relay reconnects within seconds. The recorder repairs the join, so the hour file is complete.
  • A few minutes of re-encoded audio. When a reconnect damages an audio frame, the recorder re-encodes that hour's audio instead of copying it. Picture is unaffected. The log line reads audio: aac re-encoded.
  • Second audio track in the source is silent. Phoenix sends an AC-3 track that carries digital silence. The recorder never uses it.
  • The file for the current hour is a .ts. It becomes .mp4 after the hour closes. Do not copy the .ts while it is being written.
